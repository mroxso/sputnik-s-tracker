# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Discuss facts and sources, not people. This topic is geopolitically charged; keep it civil and on topic.
- No harassment, personal attacks, trolling or publishing others' private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break these rules.

To report a problem, use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature or contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
